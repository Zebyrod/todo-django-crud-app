About TaskZap

Creator: Zebastian Rodriguez

This project is my first full-stack build since graduating from the General Assembly Software Engineering Bootcamp. TaskZap was created to reinforce ongoing Python and Django practice by rebuilding a project completely from scratch. The goal was to design something simple and useful for everyday tasks, yet flexible enough to grow with more advanced features over time. It also served as an opportunity to explore Bootstrap and strengthen front-end styling skills.

Above all, TaskZap stands as a personal challenge to keep learning, stay motivated, and continue progressing toward a future as a full-time developer.
